How to Order

Enjoy your favorite meals at Campus Bites! Follow these four simple steps to order and collect your food.

Simple 4-Step Ordering Process

Ordering food at Campus Bites is easy, quick, and convenient. Whether you are grabbing breakfast before class or having lunch with friends, follow the steps below.

📋

Step 1: Browse the Menu

Visit our Menu page and explore different food categories, including breakfast, fast food, main courses, and beverages.

View Menu

🍔

Step 2: Select Your Meal

Choose your favorite food items according to your taste and budget. Check our daily deals for affordable combos.

View Deals

💳

Step 3: Place Your Order

Visit the cafeteria counter, tell our staff your selected items, confirm your order, and make the payment.

🥗

Step 4: Collect Your Food

Wait while your meal is freshly prepared. Collect your order from the counter and enjoy your delicious food!

Important Ordering Information

⏰ Order Timings

Monday – Friday: 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM

Saturday: 9:00 AM – 3:00 PM

Sunday: Closed

💰 Payment Methods

Pay for your order directly at the cafeteria counter. Confirm the total amount before making payment.

📍 Food Collection

Collect your prepared meal from the Campus Bites cafeteria counter, Ground Floor, Student Center Building.

Quick Ordering Tips

  • Check the menu before visiting the cafeteria.
  • Look for student-friendly combo deals to save money.
  • Keep your university ID card with you.
  • Visit during less busy hours to avoid long queues.
  • Check your order before leaving the counter.
  • Keep the cafeteria area clean after eating.
